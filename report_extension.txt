BM-PD EXTENSION SWEEP: REPORT
2026-10-13

UPDATE 2026-10-23: desynchronised-update check (details in report_desync.txt).
All results below use synchronous updating (every player updates at the same moment).
Rerun with each player updating with probability 0.5 per round (84,000 runs, beta 1-100):
  - C1 (hubs defect) holds; the A > 1 reversal becomes stronger and steady
    (positive by beta = 3 on 9 of 10 networks).
  - C2 (fixed partners) holds on all 10 networks but is much smaller: about half at
    beta = 1, about a third at beta >= 10 (real - samedeg at beta = 100: +0.005 to +0.039).
  - C3: uneven degrees help, robustly (degseq - ER mostly kept). The high-beta clustering
    and structure effects keep >= 0.01 only on grqc, astroph and Facebook.
  - So the high-beta numbers below (beta >= 3) partly reflect synchronous updating.

All three experiment-1 claims hold on 5 structurally different networks, with two
refinements. Fixed partners (C2) help on all 10, but at low beta on networks with very
uneven degrees most of that effect is players keeping their degree. Structure beyond
degree (C3) barely matters at beta <= 1, but at high beta clustering adds up to +0.07
cooperation. Main regime throughout: A <= 1 (a cooperator can be satisfied); beta = 0.01
and the dead zone (a >= 0.8) are excluded, as in experiment 1.

Every number below was recomputed from the result CSVs (results/sweep_more_*,
results/band_test.csv).


1. WHAT WAS RUN
---------------

Same model and grid as experiment 1: weak PD (R = 1, T = b, S = P = 0), Bush-Mosteller
learners, averaged payoffs, synchronous updates, epsilon = 0.05, p0 = 0.5.
Grid: 12 beta (0.01-100) x 10 a (0-0.9) x 5 b (1.1-2.0) x 10 reps, 1,500 rounds,
outcome = mean cooperation over the last 500.

5 added networks (SNAP; largest component, self-loops dropped):

  Network                    Kind                          N       <k>    Degree CV  Clustering  Assortativity
  grqc (ca-GrQc)             co-authorship                 4,158    6.5   1.34       0.56        +0.64
  astroph (ca-AstroPh)       co-authorship                17,903   22.0   1.41       0.63        +0.20
  emaileu (email-Eu-core)    email                           986   32.6   1.14       0.41        -0.03
  asinternet (as20000102)    Internet routers, not social  6,474    3.9   6.44       0.25        -0.18
  gnutella (p2p-Gnutella08)  file sharing, not social      6,299    6.6   1.29       0.01        +0.04

The two non-social networks are contrasts only, never counted as social networks.

Graph types per network:
  - real, degseq (same degrees, links rewired; 5 rewirings) and shuffled (players
    re-seated at random every round), as in experiment 1.
  - samedeg (new): players re-seated every round only among seats of about the same
    degree (factor-2 degree bins, at least 10 seats per group). Loses partners, keeps
    degree. Splits real - shuffled into a fixed-partner part and a keeping-one's-degree part.
  - ER, BA, WS: same N and <k>; WS rewiring probability chosen to match the real
    clustering. One graph each.

Scale and checks: 55 new graphs x 6,000 = 330,000 runs, analysed with experiment 1's
90,000 (70 graphs). All runs present, run IDs unique, no missing values; experiment-1
runs reproduce bit-identically with the updated code. 41,937 of 42,000 cells settled;
56 still moving and 7 with disagreeing repeats (at most 4 per graph). 11% of cells
oscillate with period 2 (beta >= 2, mostly shuffled and samedeg), as in experiment 1.
Contrast 95% CIs <= 0.0023. Hypotheses H1-H7 were written before the runs.


2. C1: LOW-DEGREE PLAYERS COOPERATE MORE THAN HUBS
--------------------------------------------------

Holds on all 10 networks: Spearman(node cooperation, degree) is negative at every beta
(-0.11 to -0.77), except Facebook at beta >= 10 (-0.03 to +0.11). It is strongest at
beta = 1-2 and weakens at high beta.

  Network     beta=0.1  beta=1  beta=3  beta=10  beta=100
  Facebook     -0.57    -0.63   -0.60   -0.03    +0.11
  LastFM       -0.65    -0.77   -0.71   -0.49    -0.41
  Twitch       -0.57    -0.69   -0.67   -0.45    -0.37
  Deezer       -0.68    -0.76   -0.71   -0.51    -0.43
  GitHub       -0.48    -0.62   -0.61   -0.37    -0.28
  grqc         -0.66    -0.75   -0.60   -0.32    -0.25
  astroph      -0.65    -0.74   -0.70   -0.38    -0.27
  emaileu      -0.66    -0.68   -0.61   -0.30    -0.11
  asinternet   -0.37    -0.52   -0.46   -0.36    -0.31
  gnutella     -0.62    -0.72   -0.71   -0.54    -0.47

  Real graphs, A <= 1, mean over the regime's (a, b) cells; rep 0 only, no CIs.

- Degrees alone produce it. At beta = 0.1-3 it is negative on every comparison graph:
  degree-preserving rewiring -0.41 to -0.78 (new 5 only), BA -0.31 to -0.54,
  ER -0.18 to -0.64, WS weakest at -0.06 to -0.44.
- At high beta, real structure changes its size on some networks. At beta = 100:
  astroph real -0.27 vs rewired -0.01; emaileu -0.11 vs +0.19. On asinternet, gnutella
  and grqc the rewiring stays as negative as the real network or more.
- A > 1 (a cooperator can never be satisfied): it reverses at high beta, up to +0.71
  (asinternet, beta = 100). Where it first turns positive: beta = 2 emaileu, Facebook;
  3 GitHub, Twitch, astroph; 5 Deezer, LastFM, gnutella; 10 asinternet; 20 grqc.
  Not steady on several: positive at beta 2-3, near 0 around beta = 10, rising again
  (astroph is negative at beta 10-30, +0.09 at 100).
- a = 0: only a weak dip around beta = 1 (down to -0.32).

Mechanism test (band test), partly supported. Proposed mechanism: with share x of
cooperating neighbours, a cooperator earns x and is satisfied iff x >= a*b; a defector
earns b*x and is satisfied iff x >= a. When a <= x < a*b, defecting satisfies and
cooperating does not, and a hub's x stays near its neighbourhood mean while a
low-degree player's x jumps around. Prediction (written before running): correlation
clearly negative when the hubs' x is in that band, near 0 below or above it.
Over 3,931 non-oscillating cells (rep 0, real graphs):

  Hubs' x                   Median rho, per network   Cells with rho < -0.1
  below band (x < a)        -0.32 to -0.62            77-100%
  in band                   -0.51 to -0.82            87-100% (Facebook 66%)
  above band (x >= a*b)     -0.03 to -0.38            31-72%

The band is the most negative position on 9 of 10 networks (asinternet: band -0.51,
below -0.52), but correlations below and above it are not near 0. The band rule
contributes but does not explain C1 alone. It also says nothing about the A > 1
reversal: all those cells sit below the band.


3. C2: FIXED PARTNERS HELP, MORE AT HIGH BETA
---------------------------------------------

Holds on all 10 networks: real - shuffled is positive at every beta, from +0.006 to
+0.040 at beta = 0.1 to +0.030 to +0.108 at beta = 100. The same-degree shuffle splits
it into fixed partners (real - samedeg) and keeping one's degree (samedeg - shuffled).

              real - shuffled       real - samedeg
  Network     beta=0.1  beta=100    beta=0.1  beta=100
  Facebook    +0.008    +0.048      +0.007    +0.048
  LastFM      +0.010    +0.058      +0.006    +0.052
  Twitch      +0.021    +0.042      +0.005    +0.029
  Deezer      +0.008    +0.052      +0.006    +0.049
  GitHub      +0.029    +0.037      +0.004    +0.016
  grqc        +0.006    +0.108      +0.005    +0.104
  astroph     +0.008    +0.070      +0.006    +0.061
  emaileu     +0.011    +0.030      +0.005    -0.009
  asinternet  +0.040    +0.033      +0.002    +0.018
  gnutella    +0.014    +0.035      +0.009    +0.029

  Cooperation difference, A <= 1, paired by seed; 95% CIs <= 0.0023.

- The growth with beta is the fixed-partner part. real - samedeg rises from
  +0.002-0.009 at beta = 0.1 to +0.016-0.104 at beta = 100 on 9 of 10 networks.
  Emaileu is the exception: -0.003 to -0.010 at beta >= 10.
- At low beta on the most uneven-degree networks, most of the effect is keeping one's
  degree. At beta = 0.1: asinternet 0.037 of 0.040, GitHub 0.025 of 0.029, Twitch 0.016
  of 0.021. On the others it is at most 0.006.
- Asinternet's total effect does not grow with beta (0.027-0.040 throughout); emaileu's
  dips at beta = 2-3.
- A > 1: all contrasts small (at most +0.042).
- Not tested: whether BM gains more from fixed partners than imitators do.


4. C3: STRUCTURE BEYOND THE DEGREE SEQUENCE
-------------------------------------------

Revised: structure beyond degree barely matters at beta <= 1, but at high beta
clustering adds cooperation. At beta = 0.1-1, |real - degseq| <= 0.011 on all 10 networks.

              real - degseq            real - degseq   WS - ER     degseq - ER
  Network     max |diff|, beta 0.1-1   beta=100        beta=100    beta=1
  Facebook    0.009                    +0.013          +0.037      +0.038
  LastFM      0.002                    +0.015          +0.020      +0.043
  Twitch      0.005                    +0.005          +0.011      +0.050
  Deezer      0.001                    +0.009          +0.015      +0.033
  GitHub      0.005                    +0.000          +0.011      +0.064
  grqc        0.006                    +0.062          +0.070      +0.037
  astroph     0.008                    +0.038          +0.069      +0.047
  emaileu     0.002                    -0.011          +0.029      +0.045
  asinternet  0.011                    -0.027          +0.024      +0.051
  gnutella    0.007                    -0.007          +0.001      +0.036

  A <= 1. ER, BA and WS are one graph each, so their CIs hold run noise only.

- Clustering helps at high beta. WS - ER is within +/-0.008 at beta <= 1 and +0.011 to
  +0.070 at beta = 100, except gnutella (about 0; its WS graph is near-random). It is
  largest on the two co-authorship networks (clustering 0.56, 0.63), which also have
  the largest real - degseq (+0.062, +0.038).
- Asinternet's real network cooperates less than its rewirings at high beta (-0.027).
  Not predicted.
- Uneven degrees help: degseq - ER is +0.016 to +0.066 on all 10 networks at every
  beta, largest on GitHub.
- At low beta this matches earlier reports that reinforcement learning is largely
  insensitive to topology (Cimini & Sanchez 2014; Amaral et al. 2016). Shuffled seats
  do matter (C2); rewiring links at fixed degrees barely does at low beta.


5. HYPOTHESES: PREDICTION VS OUTCOME
------------------------------------

Five hold, H1 holds with wrong details, H6 holds only partly. All were written before
the runs (H1-H3 on 2026-10-12, H4-H7 on 2026-10-13).

H1 (C1)  Prediction: rho < 0 on all 5 new networks; weakest on grqc; reverses at A > 1,
         beta >= 3.
         Outcome: holds, details wrong: emaileu is weakest at high beta, not grqc;
         reversal starts at beta 2-20 by network.
H2 (C2)  Prediction: real - shuffled > 0 on the 5 new, grows with beta.
         Outcome: holds; asinternet flat, emaileu dips at beta 2-3.
H3 (C3)  Prediction: |real - degseq| <= 0.01 at beta <= 1; may exceed 0.024 at high beta
         on grqc, astroph.
         Outcome: holds (asinternet 0.011, borderline); grqc +0.062, astroph +0.040 at
         high beta. Not predicted: asinternet -0.027.
H4 (C3)  Prediction: degseq - ER > 0 on all 10, largest on asinternet, GitHub.
         Outcome: holds; GitHub largest, asinternet not above the rest.
H5 (C3)  Prediction: WS - ER about 0 at low beta, positive at high beta.
         Outcome: holds; gnutella about 0 as expected.
H6 (C1)  Prediction: rho < 0 on degseq and BA as on real; weaker on ER, WS.
         Outcome: partly: also clearly negative on ER; only WS clearly weaker.
H7 (C2)  Prediction: real - samedeg > 0 on all 10, grows with beta.
         Outcome: holds on 9 of 10; emaileu negative at beta >= 10.


6. CAVEATS AND NEXT STEPS
-------------------------

- Node-level correlations (C1, band test) use rep 0 only and have no CIs; emaileu's top
  1% is about 10 nodes. The original 5 networks have no node files for their rewirings
  (experiment 1 saved real graphs only).
- ER, BA and WS are one graph each.
- Asinternet and gnutella are not social networks: contrasts only.
- In the same-degree shuffle a typical player stays within about +/-20% of its group's
  mean degree, but the top group holds the 10 largest hubs, which can differ a lot
  (GitHub degrees 1,568-9,458).
- Claims are about the model on real network structure, not about real people.

Next, for the C1 mechanism: (1) one learner whose k neighbours cooperate with a fixed
probability, to see whether degree alone produces hub defection; (2) a predicted
cooperation-by-degree curve from that, compared with the simulated one. Neither has
been run.
