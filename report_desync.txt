DESYNCHRONISED-UPDATE CHECK: REPORT
2026-10-23

Most of our high-beta effects came from every player updating at the same moment. With
each player updating with probability 0.5 per round, the fixed-partner and clustering
effects at beta >= 10 keep only about 25-35% of their size, and the fixed-partner effect
already halves at beta = 1. Hub defection (C1) and the benefit of uneven degrees are
robust, and the A > 1 reversal becomes stronger and steadier. Main regime throughout:
A <= 1 (a cooperator can be satisfied), unless stated.

Every number below was recomputed from the result CSVs (results/sweep_desync_*).


1. WHAT WAS TESTED AND RUN
--------------------------

The concern. In our model every player updates its cooperation probability at the same
moment each round (synchronous updating). At high beta each update is large, so the whole
population can switch together; this lockstep is also what produced period-2 oscillations
in 11% of cells. The question: are the high-beta effects a property of the network, or of
the lockstep?

The change (mentor's code, merged 2026-10-21): a setting update_fraction. Every player
still plays and earns its payoff every round, but updates only with probability
update_fraction; the rest keep their probability. 1.0 is the old model (bit-identical);
the check uses 0.5.

The run (run.py --desync): 10 networks x 6 graph types (real, degseq, shuffled, samedeg,
ER, WS) x beta {1, 3, 10, 100} x a 0.1-0.7 x 5 b (1.1-2.0) x 10 reps = 84,000 runs,
1,500 rounds. Same seeds as the synchronous sweeps, so each run has a synchronous twin.
beta = 1 is the control. BA was left out (not used in a contrast).

Checks before running:
  - 20/20 unit tests; numpy model vs Mesa reference: 13/13 cells agree, including 4
    desync cells (difference <= 0.0003).
  - update_fraction = 1 reproduces old runs exactly: 30 old runs (all graph types,
    beta 1-100) rerun bit-identically.
  - All 84,000 desync runs have a synchronous twin; all 1,400 real-graph rep-0 cells have
    a synchronous node file.

Run quality. All 84,000 runs present, no missing values. 8,397 of 8,400 cells settled
(2 still moving, 1 with disagreeing repeats); largest mean drift at beta = 1 is 0.0085
(limit 0.02). Oscillating cells: 34 of 8,400 (0.4%), against 1,375 of the same 8,400
cells (16%) synchronously. Contrast 95% CIs <= 0.0031.

Cooperation level. Desync lowers mean cooperation at high beta. Real graphs, A <= 1
(sync -> desync): 0.412 -> 0.405 at beta = 1, 0.450 -> 0.425 at beta = 3,
0.489 -> 0.448 at beta = 10, 0.495 -> 0.450 at beta = 100.


2. H8: PREDICTION VS OUTCOME
----------------------------

One part fails, two hold. H8 was written before the runs, from the mentor's spot check
(4 networks, 3 seeds: beta = 0.3 unchanged, beta = 100 effects 40-65% smaller).

(a) Prediction: at beta = 1 every contrast changes by <= 0.005.
    Outcome: FAILS for fixed partners. real - shuffled and real - samedeg keep only
    23-82% of their size (median 54% and 45%); largest change -0.015 (grqc). Passes for
    the rest: degree spread <= 0.002, WS - ER <= 0.005, real - degseq <= 0.007 (borderline).
(b) Prediction: at beta >= 3 the contrasts shrink by about half.
    Outcome: HOLDS, and they shrink more than half. Median share kept at beta = 10 / 100:
    real - shuffled 26% / 30%, real - samedeg 32% / 33%, WS - ER 25% / 29%,
    real - degseq 44% / 46%. Degree spread (degseq - ER) is mostly kept: median 87% / 86%.
(c) Prediction: on grqc and astroph, real - samedeg and WS - ER stay > 0.01 at beta = 100.
    Outcome: HOLDS. real - samedeg: grqc +0.039, astroph +0.023. WS - ER: grqc +0.029,
    astroph +0.025.

Share kept = desync / synchronous, per network and beta, only where the synchronous
effect is >= 0.005.

6. CLAIM REVISIONS (applied 2026-10-23 in ResearchQ/question.md and README.md)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

C1  Before: low-degree players cooperate more than hubs (A <= 1); reverses at A > 1,
    high beta, from beta ~2-20, not steadily.
    Now: kept. Holds under desynchronised updating (typically 0.08 weaker); the A > 1

    Now: uneven degrees help, robustly. Structure beyond degree barely matters at

New results also reported: at A > 1, desync makes fixed partners and uneven degrees
lower cooperation at high beta (-0.003 to -0.020 and -0.017 to -0.036 at beta = 100).


7. CAVEATS AND OPEN QUESTIONS
-----------------------------

- Two changes at once. Desync breaks the lockstep and also halves how often each player
  updates (about 750 updates instead of 1,500). The runs settled, but the drop in the
  partner effect at beta = 1 cannot yet be assigned to either change. A run with
  sequential (one-at-a-time) updating, or with update_fraction = 0.5 over 3,000 rounds,
  would separate them.
- One desync level. Only update_fraction = 0.5 was run; whether the effects keep
  shrinking at lower fractions is unknown.
- Grid cut. beta = 1, 3, 10, 100 and a = 0.1-0.7 only; no a = 0, beta < 1 or dead zone,
  and BA is not included.
- Node level (C1) is rep 0 only, with no CIs. ER and WS are one graph each.
- Desync runs share run IDs with their synchronous twins; they are kept in a separate CSV
  (results/sweep_desync_runs.csv).
